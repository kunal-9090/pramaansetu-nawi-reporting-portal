# TrustGate for NAWI Reporting — Rules

## Evidence Before Result
A final report is blocked until instrument identity, test setup, required evidence, readings, calculations, reliable TrustGate validation, officer approval and an audit trail are complete.

## TrustGate checks
1. Identity completeness
2. Setup traceability
3. Evidence completeness
4. Image quality status
5. Reading availability
6. Calculation completion
7. Cross-observation consistency
8. Counter-evidence readiness
9. Officer review status

## TrustGate statuses
- **Ready for Decision:** prerequisites satisfied; officer action is next.
- **Needs Evidence:** required item missing or unusable.
- **Needs Retest:** readings/setup are inconsistent or invalid.
- **Manual Review Required:** confidence or interpretation needs human review.

## OIML applicability
Instrument profile determines Repeatability, Eccentricity, Indication Error, Tare, Creep and context-dependent Substitution. Each test is marked Applicable, Not Applicable or Needs Review.

## Case statuses
Draft, Evidence Pending, Under TrustGate Review, Officer Review, Approved, Sent for Retest, Potential Non-Compliance, Report Generated.

## Outcome statuses
Pass, Review / Retest, Potential Non-Compliance, Not Applicable.

## Report traceability
Every finding must link to a reading, evidence photo, load point, calculation, rule version, officer decision and timestamp. Rework routes are explicit: missing evidence → Evidence Capture; wrong identity → Passport; invalid setup → Test Setup; unreliable decision → Manual Review/Retest; officer rejection → Guided Test or Counter-Evidence.
